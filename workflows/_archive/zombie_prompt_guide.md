# 《我的女友是丧尸》Wan2.1 Prompt 优化指南

## 基础角色设定 (统一用于所有场景)

### 女主角 - 丧尸女友
```
A beautiful young East Asian woman with pale skin, dark circles under her eyes, 
slightly messy long black hair, wearing a torn pink dress. She has a gentle, 
loving expression despite her zombie appearance. Her movements are slightly 
僵硬 but graceful. She is a romantic zombie girlfriend who retains her human 
emotions and personality.
```

### 男主角 - 人类男友
```
A young East Asian man with short black hair, wearing casual clothes 
(t-shirt and jeans). He looks caring and protective, with a warm smile 
when looking at his zombie girlfriend.
```

### 场景氛围
```
Post-apocalyptic urban setting, abandoned buildings, overgrown vegetation, 
soft natural lighting, romantic atmosphere despite the desolate surroundings. 
The overall mood is bittersweet romance in a zombie apocalypse world.
```

## 分场景 Prompt 优化

### 第一集：相遇篇

**场景1：丧尸来袭**
```
A terrifying zombie horde shambles through a destroyed city street at dusk. 
Broken cars, shattered glass, and debris everywhere. The zombies move slowly 
with jerky motions, their clothes torn and dirty. Dark, ominous atmosphere 
with orange sunset light filtering through dust clouds. Cinematic horror 
movie style, dramatic lighting, 4K quality.
```

**场景2：躲藏求生**
```
A young East Asian man hides behind an overturned bus, holding his breath. 
He peeks through a broken window, eyes wide with fear. In the background, 
zombies wander aimlessly. Tense, suspenseful atmosphere, dim lighting, 
shallow depth of field focusing on his worried face.
```

**场景3：初次相遇**
```
In an abandoned supermarket, a young East Asian man and a beautiful zombie 
girl lock eyes across the aisle. She has pale skin, dark circles, messy hair, 
wearing a torn pink dress, but her expression is gentle and confused rather 
than aggressive. Soft beam of light from a broken skylight illuminates them. 
Romantic tension, bittersweet moment, cinematic composition.
```

**场景4：决定同行**
```
The zombie girl extends her hand to the young man, her movements slightly 
僵硬 but gentle. He hesitates, then reaches out to take her hand. Close-up 
of their hands almost touching, with soft backlight creating a silhouette 
effect. Emotional, hopeful moment in a dark world.
```

**场景5：保护时刻**
```
A group of aggressive zombies approaches. The zombie girl steps in front 
of the young man protectively, spreading her arms wide. She growls at the 
other zombies to scare them away. Dramatic lighting, heroic pose, 
showing her loyalty and love despite being a zombie herself.
```

### 第二集：相处篇

**场景6：一起逛街**
```
The zombie girlfriend and human boyfriend walk hand-in-hand through an 
abandoned shopping mall. She wears a cute hat to hide her zombie features. 
They look at broken store windows together. Sweet, romantic atmosphere, 
soft natural light from skylights, showing love persists even in apocalypse.
```

**场景7：学习使用手机**
```
In a cozy corner of an abandoned building, the zombie girl tries to use 
a smartphone. Her movements are clumsy but determined. The young man 
teaches her patiently, pointing at the screen. Warm, domestic scene, 
soft lighting from candles, showing their normal life together.
```

**场景8：分享食物**
```
The young man offers a can of food to his zombie girlfriend. She looks 
at it curiously, then tries to eat but struggles. He helps her gently. 
Intimate moment, soft focus on their interaction, warm candlelight, 
showing care and tenderness in their unusual relationship.
```

**场景9：夜晚守候**
```
The zombie girl sits by a window at night, watching over her sleeping 
boyfriend. Moonlight illuminates her pale face as she keeps guard 
protectively. Peaceful, romantic atmosphere, beautiful moonlight 
composition, showing her devotion and love.
```

**场景10：温馨时刻**
```
The couple sits together by a campfire in a safe hideout. She rests 
her head on his shoulder, both looking at the stars through a broken 
roof. Cozy, intimate moment, warm firelight mixed with cool moonlight, 
finding beauty and love in a broken world.
```

### 第三集：危机篇

**场景11：丧尸围攻**
```
A massive horde of aggressive zombies surrounds their safe house. 
The zombies claw at the doors and windows, trying to get in. Dark, 
threatening atmosphere, dramatic shadows, intense action scene 
with the couple trapped inside.
```

**场景12：保护反击**
```
The zombie girlfriend stands at the doorway, facing the horde alone. 
She lets out a powerful scream that makes the other zombies hesitate. 
She protects her boyfriend with her own body. Heroic, dramatic 
lighting, powerful pose showing her strength and love.
```

**场景13：受伤时刻**
```
During the escape, the young man gets scratched by a zombie. The 
zombie girl panics, tears in her eyes as she bandages his wound 
with her torn dress. Emotional, close-up on their worried faces, 
dramatic lighting showing the danger they face.
```

**场景14：病毒危机**
```
The young man falls ill from the infection, tossing and turning 
with fever. The zombie girl stays by his side, wiping his forehead 
with a cool cloth, refusing to leave him. Tender, emotional scene, 
soft lighting, showing her unwavering devotion.
```

**场景15：找到解药**
```
In an abandoned hospital, the zombie girl finds a vial of medicine. 
She holds it up to the light, hope in her eyes. Dramatic backlight 
creates a halo effect around the medicine vial. Moment of hope 
and relief, cinematic lighting.
```

### 第四集：重生篇

**场景16：解药生效**
```
The young man's fever breaks. He opens his eyes to see his zombie 
girlfriend watching over him with tears of joy. Soft morning light 
filters through broken windows. Emotional reunion, warm lighting, 
showing relief and happiness after the crisis.
```

**场景17：互相依偎**
```
The couple sits together, holding each other close after the ordeal. 
She leans into him, he wraps his arms protectively around her. 
Intimate, tender moment, soft focus on their connection, 
beautiful composition showing their deep bond.
```

**场景18：许下承诺**
```
Under a cherry blossom tree in an abandoned park, the young man 
kneels and holds out a ring made from a silver spoon. The zombie 
girl covers her mouth in surprise, tears streaming down her face. 
Romantic, beautiful scene, petals falling around them, 
cinematic proposal moment.
```

**场景19：夕阳婚礼**
```
The couple exchanges vows in an abandoned church at sunset. 
Golden light streams through stained glass windows. She wears 
a makeshift wedding dress, he wears a suit jacket. Beautiful, 
romantic ceremony, warm sunset colors, showing love conquers all.
```

**场景20：新世界希望**
```
The couple stands together on a rooftop at dawn, looking out 
over the city. The sun rises, casting golden light over the 
ruins. They hold hands, ready to face the future together. 
Hopeful, beautiful composition, epic scale, showing love 
and hope in a new world.
```

## 风格关键词库

### 整体风格
- `cinematic, movie still, film grain`
- `dramatic lighting, golden hour, blue hour`
- `shallow depth of field, bokeh`
- `professional photography, 4K, high quality`

### 情感氛围
- `bittersweet romance, emotional, tender`
- `hopeful, optimistic despite apocalypse`
- `intimate, loving, protective`
- `beautiful sadness, poetic`

### 视觉效果
- `soft natural light, warm tones`
- `cool moonlight, blue shadows`
- `golden sunset, orange highlights`
- `candlelight, cozy atmosphere`

### 角色表现
- `gentle expression, loving eyes`
- `slightly awkward but graceful`
- `protective, caring, devoted`
- `vulnerable yet strong`

## 使用建议

1. **保持一致性**：每个场景都包含基础角色设定关键词
2. **情感连贯**：按照剧情发展调整情感关键词
3. **风格统一**：始终使用 `cinematic, movie still` 等风格词
4. **质量控制**：添加 `4K, high quality, detailed` 等质量词
