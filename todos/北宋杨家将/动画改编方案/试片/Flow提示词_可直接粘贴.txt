Monochrome ink-wash image, black white and grey, drawn like an old woodblock illustration. A weathered stone stele stands on a bare hillside. Before it a lone warrior in broken armour, back to camera, facing the stone, small within the frame. Beside him a long sword is planted upright in the ground, its tassel the only cinnabar-red note in the entire image. Sparse snow drifts down. Camera rises very slowly from behind the figure, passing over his shoulder toward the stele. Vast empty sky above. Every value sits as one flat shape of ink, the way an old woodblock print is cut. SFX: wind, then one low short dull impact like wood striking stone, then long wind.

Flow 写法六条纪律：
1. 不写否定句（Flow 无负向提示词字段，Google 明确建议避免否定）
2. 运镜词前置
3. 一段只给一个运镜
4. 想慢就写 slowly / gradually / very slowly；不要出现 fast / quick / rapid
5. 提示词 100-150 词为上限
6. 主体名词放前面，不要并列多个主体
