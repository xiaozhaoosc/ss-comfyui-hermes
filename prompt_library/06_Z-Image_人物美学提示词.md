## Prompt 通用公式

```
构图(Framing) → 主体(Subject) → 神态(Expression)
→ 服饰(Fabric Detail) → 场景(Setting) → 光线(Lighting)
→ 技术标签(Technical Tags)
```

**核心要点**：
- **中英混合**：中文描述意境/感觉，英文补充精确参数
- **细节极致**：写到毛孔可见、面料光泽、烟雾中的光线粒子级别
- **光线是灵魂**：每条 Prompt 至少 3-4 种光线描述（主光 + 边光 + 补光 + 反光）

```

poetic, cinematic and atmospheric vibe

natural glowing skin texture, visible pores

sitting gracefully on textured coastal rocks
身体背对着镜头
seen from behind, back to camera
优雅地转头越过肩膀看向镜头
turning head elegantly, looking over her shoulder at the viewer
深情而富有表现力的眼睛
soulful and expressive eyes

strong sea breeze blowing her long hair, dynamic flying hair strands framing her face
```
